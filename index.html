About Bharathapuzha

Bharathapuzha, popularly known as Nila River, is one of the major rivers of Kerala, India. It originates in the Western Ghats and flows westward through parts of Tamil Nadu and Kerala before joining the Arabian Sea near Ponnani.

Bharathapuzha is the second-longest river in Kerala and plays an important role in the geographical, agricultural, ecological and cultural development of the region.

The river basin includes several tributaries, reservoirs, irrigation projects and important water resources. Malampuzha Dam, Kanjirapuzha Dam and Mangalam Dam are important water infrastructure projects in the wider basin region.

The river is also closely connected with Kerala's history, literature and cultural traditions. The banks of Bharathapuzha have inspired many poets, writers and artists.

This Interactive WebGIS project provides a geographical platform to explore the Bharathapuzha river basin, understand its physical features and examine important water resources through interactive mapping.
